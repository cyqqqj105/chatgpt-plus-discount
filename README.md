## Plus versus Pro 5x and Pro 20x

The higher-tier products are designed for users who need more capacity, but more expensive access is not automatically better for every workflow.

### ChatGPT Plus

Plus is the sensible starting point for most individuals. It covers common tasks such as writing, coding, research, image generation, and document analysis. If your main complaint is occasionally hitting the free plan’s limits, Plus is usually the first upgrade to evaluate.

### ChatGPT Pro 5x

Pro 5x is aimed at heavier usage. WildAI’s help center gives it a separate activation flow and describes it as a distinct ChatGPT product. The process may require more sensitive account information than the Plus workflow, including a session JSON and browser cookie.

That additional requirement is not a small detail. A browser cookie can represent an active logged-in session. Anyone considering Pro 5x should understand what information is being requested, how long it will be retained, and how it can be revoked.

### ChatGPT Pro 20x

Pro 20x is the highest WildAI ChatGPT product documented in its public help center. Availability is not guaranteed. WildAI specifically notes that restrictions may affect whether new Pro 20x subscriptions or upgrades are available at a given time.

For most users, Pro 20x only makes financial sense when the usage limits of Plus or a lower Pro tier are a real operational problem. Buying a higher tier because the name sounds more powerful is an expensive way to discover that your actual bottleneck was payment, workflow design, or account availability.

- You should never submit your password, one-time verification code, recovery code, or two-factor authentication secret.
- Do not paste credentials into an unfamiliar domain or browser extension.
- Do not reuse a work account if company policy prohibits third-party access.
- After the subscription is complete, review active sessions and account security settings.

## Renewal and multi-month subscriptions

Multi-month pricing can look attractive because the average monthly cost is lower. The mechanism matters, though.

WildAI’s help center says long-term plans may involve monthly renewals after the user prepays for multiple months. It also warns that cancelling the subscription through the official ChatGPT page can stop future renewals and may affect prepaid months.

Before selecting a multi-month option, confirm:

1. Whether the order is a one-time purchase or an automatic monthly renewal.
2. Whether the next month is funded in advance or charged again later.
3. Whether the platform needs a new session or cookie for future renewals.
4. What happens if a later renewal fails.
5. Whether unused months can be refunded.
6. Where cancellation must be performed.
7. Whether the displayed amount includes all service fees.

Do not assume that a three-month third-party order is identical to an official OpenAI three-month prepaid plan. OpenAI currently describes ChatGPT Plus as a monthly subscription and says annual billing or paying several months in advance is not available for Plus.

## Refund rules to check before buying

WildAI advertises a refund for failed subscriptions on its public site, but its help center also says that completed orders with active benefits generally are not refundable for personal reasons such as choosing the wrong product, making an accidental purchase, or changing your mind.

That means “full refund if subscription fails” should not be read as “every unwanted order can be refunded.”

Before payment, distinguish between:

- The platform failed to activate the subscription.
- The account activated but later became restricted.
- The wrong email or account was submitted.
- The wrong product or duration was purchased.
- A multi-month renewal failed.
- Payment succeeded but the order remains pending.
- The subscription worked and the user simply no longer wants it.

These cases may be handled differently. Keep the order page, payment receipt, and product description. If the service is not activated, contact WildAI support through the order system instead of opening a duplicate order.
